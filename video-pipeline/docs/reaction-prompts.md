# Reaction set for Sam and Maya: the Gamma prompts

Twelve drawings, one batch, so the line weight matches. Each costs 70 Gamma
credits; the batch needs 840. Generate them with the `generate_image` tool,
type "illustration", square, then attach the results here and they go
through `illus-trace.py`, the manifest and the catalogue like the rest.

Style line, the same every time:

> Simple clean black line art illustration, black outlines only on a pure
> white background, no colour, no fill, no shading, no grey, no background
> scenery, uniform medium line weight, cartoon vector style like a whiteboard
> explainer. Head and shoulders only, facing the viewer.

Sam: "a young man with short wavy dark hair and a plain t-shirt".
Maya: "a young woman with long straight hair and a crew-neck jumper".

| name | prompt tail |
|---|---|
| sam-worried | Sam, brow furrowed, biting his lip, one hand on the back of his neck |
| sam-surprised | Sam, eyebrows up, mouth open, hands raised |
| sam-pleased | Sam, grinning, thumbs up |
| sam-counting | Sam, counting a small fan of banknotes, tongue out in concentration |
| sam-thinking | Sam, chin on his fist, eyes up, a small question mark above |
| sam-facepalm | Sam, palm over his face, shoulders slumped |
| maya-worried | Maya, brow furrowed, hands clasped under her chin |
| maya-surprised | Maya, eyebrows up, mouth open, hand on her cheek |
| maya-pleased | Maya, grinning, fist pumped |
| maya-counting | Maya, dropping a coin into her palm, counting |
| maya-thinking | Maya, finger on her chin, eyes up, a small question mark above |
| maya-facepalm | Maya, palm over her face, eyes closed |

Once in the library: `react=sam-worried` on any scene puts it small beside
the words (it is `art=` with a fixed place and size), and the studio's
library gets a Reactions chip.
