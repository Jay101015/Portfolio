# What the animated money channels do, and what we take from it

A study of the faceless, drawn-on-screen personal finance channels, done to
steer the illustrated style. Web pages could not be opened from this
environment (only search summaries), so the channel facts below come from
search results and from what is known of the channels; watch one video from
each before leaning on a detail.

## The channels

| Channel | What it is | What it does on screen |
|---|---|---|
| The Swedish Investor (~1M) | Book summaries and investing ideas, "summed up in 12 minutes", numbered takeaways | A recurring cartoon investor who reacts to the point (worried, delighted, holding a money bag); drawn charts that build as the voice walks through them; one or two big words per beat in colour; a joke drawing every couple of minutes; a recap of the takeaways at the end; calm music bed |
| WhiteBoard Finance (~1M) | Marko at a real whiteboard, long explainers | Diagram built up over the whole video, boxes and arrows, numbers written large; conversational, no cuts |
| Chris Invests (~160K) | VideoScribe whiteboard animations, written and drawn by one person | Icons drawn as named; sentences written as spoken; lists; low cost, several million-view videos |
| Two Cents (PBS) | Two hosts plus 2D animation inserts, 6 to 10 minutes | Question, a bit of history, the behavioural trap, three things to do; humour; short; "and that's our two cents" sign-off |
| Practical Wisdom (~1M) | Self-improvement and money listicles with narration | Flat animated figures, a heading card per point, calm narration, numbered points |
| After Skool | Real hand drawing time-lapsed over a speech | Picture-first: the drawing is the content, words are sparse |

## What they have in common

1. **A number in the hook.** "A 1% fee costs you a third", "the 7 books ranked", "summed up in 12 minutes". The first ten seconds prove the title with one real number; nobody opens with a logo.
2. **Numbered sections.** "Takeaway 1 of 5" cards with a big drawn number. The viewer always knows where they are, and it is the re-hook every 60 to 90 seconds that retention guides keep pointing at.
3. **One character, many moods.** The same drawn person turns up in every video and reacts: worried, surprised, pleased, counting money. The reaction is the joke and the emotional beat.
4. **Words are emphasis, not transcript.** One to three words per beat, large, often in colour (red for losses, green for gains). Chris Invests writes full sentences and it is the weakest-looking of the set.
5. **Charts drawn live.** A line or bars that grow while the voice says the numbers. Every one of these channels does it; it is the money niche's signature shot.
6. **A recap at the end.** The takeaways listed again, drawn as a checklist, before the sign-off.
7. **Sound.** Marker scratch while drawing, a soft bed of music, a small sound on a reveal. Silence reads as unfinished.
8. **Length and titles.** Long-form 8 to 15 minutes (the Swedish Investor now also cuts 40 to 60 minute compilations of old videos). Titles carry a number and a promise. Shorts are 30 to 60 seconds with burned-in captions.

## What this means for the illustrated style

Mapped to the pipeline, in the order that moves the most:

1. **Takeaway cards.** A `[takeaway n="1" of="5" title="..."]` scene: the big number drawn, the title written, the board clears. The studio drafts them from the script's headings. Doubles as the re-hook.
2. **A reaction set for Sam and Maya.** Six drawings each in the same style: worried, surprised, pleased, counting money, thinking, facepalm. Used by `react=` on any scene, drawn small beside the words, so a point can land on a face. One Gamma batch.
3. **Picture-first frame.** The traced drawing at 70 percent of the board height, words in a tighter, larger column, and a hand-written board line per scene instead of five picked words. (Already on the list.)
4. **Colour emphasis.** The key number or word of a scene in the accent colour, red for a loss, green for a gain, picked by sign; one filled area per drawing in the accent colour.
5. **Recap scene.** `[recap]` writes the takeaways as a checklist, ticked one by one, before the outro.
6. **Sound bed.** Marker scratch under drawing, a soft music bed with ducking under the voice, a small tick on each board clear. Rendered in, optional per video.
7. **Hook template.** The studio's draft flow asks for the one number the video proves and writes the first scene around it.
8. **Shorts cut.** The vertical render with burned-in captions and the first takeaway only.
