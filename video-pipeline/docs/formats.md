# No clones: every video gets its own shape and its own look

Jeffrey, after the retirement video: "don't always make them the exact same way, they're all starting to look like
clones." Every long video so far has used one spine: stat hook -> teaser board -> title card -> "Takeaway 1 of N"
chapter cards -> stat / footage / board on repeat -> recap -> outro. The drawings change, the shape does not.

## The rule

Before a script is written, pick and record here, in the ledger below:
1. **A format** from the list that none of the last two videos used.
2. **A signature device**: one visual idea that runs through this video and no other (a ladder that fills rung by
   rung, a running tally, a split-screen race, a calendar that tears off, a receipt that keeps printing...).
3. **A chapter device**: how the viewer knows where they are. "Takeaway n of N" cards are one option, not the default;
   never two videos in a row.
4. **An opening shape**: stat-first hooks are one option. Also: a question to the viewer, a cold open on the reveal, a
   personal admission, a "pause and guess" test.
5. **An ending shape**: recap list, a single reveal, a challenge to the viewer, a cliffhanger to the next video.

The director (PLAN mode) checks the ledger and refuses a plan that repeats the last video's format, signature device,
chapter device or opening. Shorts follow the same rule across a set: no two Shorts in a set share their main picture.

## Formats

- **Explainer with chapters**: the current default. Use at most one video in three.
- **The ladder / countdown**: climb (or count down) rungs; each rung is a reveal; no chapter cards, the ladder is the map.
- **Head to head**: two people, two choices, two pots, run side by side the whole way (split screen); a scoreboard.
- **The experiment**: "I tested X for N years of data"; set the rules, run it, the results reveal at the end.
- **Follow one person**: a named example (Sam, 27, £32K) whose life the numbers happen to; their story is the spine.
- **Myth list**: N things people believe, each struck through and corrected; a running tally of myths busted.
- **Timeline**: a year-by-year or age-by-age walk (25, 30, 35...), a calendar or age counter as the device.
- **Jeffrey's own numbers**: his real situation as the spine (no balances or holdings values on screen).
- **Answer the comments**: viewers' questions from earlier videos, one per section.
