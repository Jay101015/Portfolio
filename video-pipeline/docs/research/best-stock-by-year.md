# Best-performing S&P 500 stock by year, 2000 to 2026 YTD

Research note for The Everyday Investor script. Compiled 31 October 2026.

**How to read this table**

- *S&P 500 TR* = calendar-year total return of the index with dividends reinvested. Source is Slickcharts (slickcharts.com/sp500/returns/details). The Slickcharts site itself was unreachable from this session, so the figures were confirmed through search-engine snippets of the Slickcharts page and corroborating sites (YCharts, maisretorno, US500). 2026 is year to date.
- *Best stock* = the index member with the highest **price** return for the calendar year, as reported by a contemporaneous year-end source (Forbes, CNN Money, Fortune, Bloomberg, Reuters, CNBC, Motley Fool, etc.). Retrospective screens such as StatMuse were **not** used for the winner because they apply today's index membership to past years (they name LabCorp for 2000, Axon for 2003/2004, Tractor Supply for 2002, First Solar for 2007, Dollar Tree for 2008, Tesla for 2013, Block for 2017 - none were members at the time).
- *Next year* = that same stock's price return in the following calendar year (year-end close to year-end close, split-adjusted). Where a source only gave total return (with dividends), that is flagged.
- *Confidence*: **solid** = two or more agreeing sources or a primary filing; **approximate** = a single secondary source, or sources disagree by a few points; **unverified** = contested or only a weak source. "not found" means I could not source the number and have not guessed it.
- Fetching was blocked for almost every finance host in this session (Slickcharts, NYU Stern, Wikipedia, Visual Capitalist, StatMuse, CNN Money, Forbes, Macrotrends, 1stock1, YCharts, SEC EDGAR and others returned ENOTFOUND). Numbers therefore come from search-result snippets of those pages, and every row says which page.

## Rows where sources disagree, or where the winner is not settled

- **2000.** Visual Capitalist's S&P DJI-sourced list shows Dynegy at +361% for 2000, but Forbes (Dec 2001) says Dynegy rose 218% that year and was the *second*-best S&P 500 stock, and 1stock1's split-adjusted table gives +222.7%. I could not find who Forbes ranked first. A 2001 PlanSponsor/Instinet piece called Enron "last year's performance king" (Enron rose ~87% in 2000, so that is probably a different metric). StatMuse names LabCorp (+377%), Quest (+361%) and Vertex (+303%), none of which were members in 2000. Treat 2000 as unresolved; Dynegy is the safest name to use with the "second-best, +218%" wording.
- **2002.** No contemporaneous S&P-500-only ranking found. Forbes' large-cap list (Crown Cork & Seal +156%, MEMC +119%) is not restricted to the index; StatMuse's Tractor Supply (+120%) was not a member. Boston Scientific (~+77 to 87%) is the best confirmed member found, but I cannot say it was number one.
- **2004.** Autodesk (+209 to 212%) versus Apple (+199 to 201%). Both were members all year. Winner by price is Autodesk; some write-ups focus on Apple because of the iPod story.
- **2005.** CNN's 27-Dec snapshot put Apple (+131%) above Valero (+128%), but full-year figures have Valero (~+128-129%) ahead of Apple (~+122%). Treat Valero as the full-year winner with approximate confidence.
- **2007.** Retrospective screens show First Solar (+785%) and Mosaic (+337-342%), but First Solar joined the index in Oct 2009 and Mosaic in Sept 2011. Among members, CNBC named National Oilwell Varco (+143%) the top gainer at year-end; Jacobs Engineering was ~+132%.
- **2008.** Family Dollar's gain is quoted as 27% (CNBC, from 2 Jan), 38.5% (CNN) and 38.6% (Benzinga). Dollar Tree (+61 to 64%) beats it in retrospective screens but joined the index in Dec 2011.
- **2010.** Netflix's 2010 gain ranges from 216.5% (StatMuse) to 227% (CNBC/IBTimes, written 30 Dec). Year-end-to-year-end price return is about 219%.
- **2011.** Cabot's gain is 102% (Globe and Mail), 106% (CNBC) or 128.8% (Bloomberg, total return to 2 Dec).
- **2017.** NRG (+132%) beats Align (+131%) on price; Benzinga (+135.6%) and Forbes (total return) put Align first. Both are in the table.
- **2018-2019.** AMD's gains are quoted anywhere from 77% to 80% (2018) and 146.6% to 154.6% (2019) depending on the data vendor and cut-off date.
- **2020.** Tesla (+743%) was a member for only 7 trading days of 2020; Etsy (~+300%, joined Sept 2020) is the best near-full-year member.
- **2021.** Devon: ~+178-179% price vs ~+192-194% total return (large variable dividend).
- **2025.** Sandisk only began trading in Feb 2025 and joined the index on 28 Nov 2025, so its "2025 return" is from the debut, not from a 31 Dec 2024 close; quoted as 559% to 596%.
- **Index returns.** 2000-2012 figures were confirmed to one decimal place only (search snippet of Slickcharts); 2013-2025 confirmed to two decimals against Slickcharts/YCharts/maisretorno. 2026 YTD: Slickcharts 13.82% and chartrow 13.70%, both to 29 Oct 2026; the index fell ~1.8% on 30 Oct.
