Worked figures (2026-27 England tax; pension income has no National Insurance):
- Gross income for the spending target: moderate £37,732; comfortable £54,720. Less the state pension: £25,185 and £42,172 a year from the pot.
- Pot at the 4% rule (x25): moderate about £630,000; comfortable about £1.05 million. Couple, each: moderate about £317,000, comfortable about £587,000.
- A renter on a moderate budget plus £1,406 a month rent (ONS, October 2026): about £1.23 million.
- Monthly to reach £630,000 by 67 at 4% a year after inflation: from 25 £483; from 35 £811; from 45 £1,492. For £1.05 million: £804; £1,352; £2,487.
- Fees: £300 a month for 30 years at 7% before fees: 0.3% fees about £345,000, 1% about £301,000 (gap about £43,700).
- Caveats said in the video: the 4% rule is a rough guide (some UK research says 3.5%); the 25% tax-free lump sum lowers the tax, so the pots are a little high; the figures are illustrations.
