## GTA 6: how to invest in it, and make money from it (timed: before 19 November 2026)

**Window.** GTA 6 comes out 19 November 2026 (PS5, Xbox Series X|S). Take-Two reports earnings on 9 November. The video
has to go up before the 19th to ride the hype; the earnings on the 9th may change the share price story, so record
after them if possible, or keep the share-price lines easy to re-record.

**Angle.** Investing in GTA (Rockstar's owner Take-Two, ticker TTWO), the ways people make money from the game, and the
outside-the-box money lessons it teaches. For the viewer: "should you buy before GTA 6?"

**Shape (not scripted yet).**
- Hook: £1,000 in GTA's maker on GTA 5's launch day is about £13,000 now. Stakes: the £40 you spent on the game, put into
  the company instead, is about £525. Twist: the shares are down over a quarter since July, weeks before GTA 6.
- Teasers: why the price falls while pre-orders break records; two ways people make real money from the game and the
  scam already taking people's money; at the end, what happened to the shares the week GTA 5 came out.
- 1. Own the maker: you can't buy Rockstar, you buy Take-Two (US-listed, in an ISA, a currency fee). It's a bet on one game.
- 2. You may already own it: S&P 500 and global trackers. The picks-and-shovels play: Sony and Microsoft take a cut of
  digital sales (check the figure).
- 3. Money from the game: content (the launch weeks are the biggest moment in gaming), roleplay servers and scripts; the
  scam to avoid.
- 4. What GTA teaches about money: GTA 5's in-game stock market missions are insider dealing, a crime in the UK. In real
  life, by the time you hear the news it's in the price.
- Personal beat: what Jeffrey is doing (his call, not written for him).
- Reveal: GTA 5 made a billion in 3 days and the shares went down that week. The launch didn't make people rich; holding did.
