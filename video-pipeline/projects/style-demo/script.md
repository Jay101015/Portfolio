That's a gap of over £32,000, every single year, for twenty years or more. And the only thing that fills it is you.

So how much do you actually need? I'll show you what three different retirements cost, what you'd need to put away a month starting at 25, 35 or 45, the three things that make it a lot cheaper, and at the end, whether you really need a million pounds. The answer depends on one thing most people never think about.

Let's start with what retirement actually costs, because most people have never seen the number.

[broll search="retired couple walking on british seaside promenade" caption="Three ways to retire" cards="ink:Pensions UK@Pensions; ink:minimum, moderate, comfortable@minimum; ink:spending budgets, after tax@after; ink:outside London@outside" src="06-retired-couple-walking-on-british-seasid.mp4" credit="Video 152798 by ghasoub via Pixabay"]
Every year, Pensions UK, the body that represents UK pension schemes, works out what retirement costs at three levels: minimum, moderate and comfortable. These are spending budgets, after tax, for someone living outside London.

[set room=living-room walk cards="ink:£13,900 a year, one person@13,900; ink:food, bills, a week's holiday in the UK@food; red:if something breaks, it hurts@breaks"]
The minimum is about {count-notes} £13,900 a year for one person. That covers the {count-fingers} basics: food, bills, a week's holiday in the UK, not much else. {prop=envelope} If something {bill-shock} breaks, it hurts.

[stat value="£32,700" label="A moderate retirement, one person, a year" sub="a holiday abroad, a car, some security" source="Pensions UK Retirement Living Standards, June 2026, outside London" art=emergency-jar art_at=r art_size=0.5 cards="green:a holiday abroad each year@abroad; ink:running a car@car; ink:eating out now and then@eating; green:not panicking when the boiler goes@boiler"]
Moderate is about £32,700. That gets you more security: a holiday abroad each year, running a car, eating out now and then, and not panicking when the boiler goes.

[stat value="£45,400" label="A comfortable retirement, one person, a year" sub="regular holidays, help for family, real freedom" source="Pensions UK Retirement Living Standards, June 2026, outside London" keep=arrow art=retired-bench art_at=r art_size=0.5 cards="green:regular holidays abroad@regular; ink:a newer car@newer; green:helping your kids or grandkids@helping; green:real freedom with your time@freedom"]
And comfortable is about £45,400. That's regular holidays abroad, a newer car, helping your kids or grandkids, and real freedom with your time.
