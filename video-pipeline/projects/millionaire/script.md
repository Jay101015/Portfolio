Four hundred and twenty pounds a month for forty years makes you a millionaire. No lottery, no crypto. Here is how it actually works.

[takeaway title="Start now, not when you're ready"]
Takeaway one. Start now, not when you are ready. Time is the part you cannot buy back.

[text board="Payday is the moment"]
Sam gets paid on the twenty-eighth. The money that goes into investments on payday is the only money that is guaranteed to get there.

[text board="The first £100k is the hard bit"]
The first hundred thousand is the hard bit. Sam climbs the stairs one coin stack at a time, and for years it feels like nothing is happening.

[takeaway title="£420 a month, every month"]
Takeaway two. Four hundred and twenty pounds a month, every month, into a cheap index fund in a stocks and shares ISA.

[text board="Cut the leaks first"]
Where does four hundred and twenty come from? Maya found hers in the subscriptions. Twelve of them, nearly two hundred a month, half of them forgotten.

[grow from="£420" per=month years=40 rate=7% label="£420 a month at 7%, forty years"]
Here is what four hundred and twenty a month does at seven percent a year. Slow for a decade. Then it is not slow at all. Forty years in, it crosses one million pounds.

[takeaway title="Leave it alone"]
Takeaway three. Leave it alone. The snowball only grows while it is rolling.

[text board="It grows while you sleep"]
After year twenty, your money earns more in a year than you put in. It grows while you sleep. That is the whole trick, and it is boring, and it works.

[recap]
So. Start now. Four hundred and twenty a month, every month. Leave it alone.
