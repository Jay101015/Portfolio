**Hook**

Ten thousand pounds just landed in your account. Here's exactly what to do with it, in order.

The debt to kill first, the account that makes the growth tax-free, and at the end, the thousand pounds the government will add if you ask.

**Protect it**

First, protect it from yourself.

Do nothing for thirty days. Most of the dumb spending happens in the first week, when it still feels like free money.

Got credit card debt? Clear it before anything else. Every thousand pounds on a card at twenty percent costs you two hundred a year. Paying it off is a guaranteed twenty percent return, and nothing else on this list beats that.

Next, three months of essentials, rent, bills, food, in an easy-access savings account. Boring. It's the money that stops a broken boiler becoming a credit card bill.

**Make it grow**

Now the part that actually makes you money.

Every year you can put up to twenty thousand pounds into an ISA, and you never pay tax on what it makes. Ten thousand fits with room to spare. A stocks and shares ISA, one global index fund, done.

Left alone at seven percent a year, it's about nineteen thousand seven hundred in ten years, thirty-eight thousand seven hundred in twenty, and seventy-six thousand in thirty. You do nothing. Time does it.

And it takes about ten minutes on your phone to open one.

**The free £1,000**

And the bit most people miss.

Buying your first home one day? Put four thousand of it in a Lifetime ISA and the government adds a thousand on top. Twenty-five percent, free, for anyone eighteen to thirty-nine. That's the easiest thousand pounds you will ever make.

And spend a thousand on something you'll actually remember. You earned it. A plan you enjoy is a plan you keep.

**Recap**

Thirty days of nothing, kill the expensive debt, three months in reach, the rest into an ISA, and the free thousand if a home is in your future.
