[takeaway title="Protect it" n="1" of="3"]
First, protect it from yourself.

[text board="Rule one: sit on it for 30 days" anim=calendar-flip art_at=r art_size=0.5 dur=7 cards="ink:30 days@thirty; red:the dumb spending: week one@week"]
Do nothing for thirty days. Most of the dumb spending happens in the first week, when it still feels like free money.

[bite value="£1,000" lose="£200" label="£1,000 on a card at 20%, one year" sub="gone in interest" source="illustrative" cards="red:clear it before anything else@anything"]
Got credit card debt? Clear it before anything else. Every thousand pounds on a card at twenty percent costs you two hundred a year.

[text board="Paying it off = a guaranteed 20% return" art=card-scissors cards="green:20%, guaranteed@guaranteed"]
Paying it off is a guaranteed twenty percent return, and nothing else on this list beats that.

[text board="3 months of essentials | Easy-access savings | The money you never touch" anim=coins-jar art_at=r art_size=0.5 dur=7 cards="ink:rent + bills + food@essentials; green:easy access@easy"]
Next, three months of essentials, rent, bills, food, in an easy-access savings account. Boring. It's the money that stops a broken boiler becoming a credit card bill.

[takeaway title="Make it grow" n="2" of="3"]
Now the part that actually makes you money.

[stat value="£20,000" label="ISA allowance, every year" sub="you keep every penny it makes" source="GOV.UK, 2026" anim=isa-shield art_at=r art_size=0.6]
Every year you can put up to twenty thousand pounds into an ISA, and you never pay tax on what it makes.

[text board="£10,000 fits, with room to spare" art=index-fund-basket cards="ink:a stocks and shares ISA@stocks; green:one global index fund, done@done"]
Ten thousand fits with room to spare. A stocks and shares ISA, one global index fund, done.

[chart line title="£10,000 at 7% a year, left alone" y="£" x="Year" source="7% a year, compounded, illustrative" cards="ink:year 10: £19,700@ten; green:year 20: £38,700@twenty; green:year 30: £76,000@thirty"]

Left alone at seven percent a year, it's about nineteen thousand seven hundred in ten years, thirty-eight thousand seven hundred in twenty, and seventy-six thousand in thirty. You do nothing. Time does it.

[broll lib="businessman-phone-city" caption="Ten minutes on your phone" marks="green:circle 46,82@ten" cards="green:open an ISA: 10 minutes@minutes; ink:on your phone@phone"]
And it takes about ten minutes on your phone to open one.

[takeaway title="The free £1,000" n="3" of="3"]
And the bit most people miss.

[stat value="£1,000" label="Lifetime ISA bonus, at most, a year" sub="25% on up to £4,000" source="GOV.UK, 2026"]
Buying your first home one day? Put four thousand of it in a Lifetime ISA and the government adds a thousand on top.

[text board="Free money, for anyone 18 to 39" art=lisa-bonus cards="green:25% on top@percent; ink:the easiest £1,000 you'll ever make@make"]
Twenty-five percent, free, for anyone eighteen to thirty-nine. That's the easiest thousand pounds you will ever make.

[text board="£1,000 for you. Seriously." art=airport-departures art_at=r art_size=0.5 cards="green:you earned it@earned; ink:a plan you enjoy is a plan you keep@enjoy"]
And spend a thousand on something you'll actually remember. You earned it. A plan you enjoy is a plan you keep.
