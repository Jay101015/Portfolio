## What counts as free use here

Public domain and CC0 (no conditions), CC BY and CC BY-SA (attribution shown on screen, which the credit= tag does), and the Pexels licence. CC BY-NC and CC BY-ND are skipped: a monetised channel is commercial use, and a review is a derivative. Trailers and studio clips are never free use; using them is a fair-use judgement and a Content ID question, covered in docs/monetisation-plan.md.
