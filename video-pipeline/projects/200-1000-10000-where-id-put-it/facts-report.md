## The catalogue

`fact=` takes a key or plain words (base rate, us inflation):

- uk.cpi: UK inflation (CPI) (ONS)
- uk.cpih: UK inflation (CPIH) (ONS)
- uk.base-rate: Bank of England base rate (Bank of England)
- uk.mortgage-2y: Average 2-year fixed mortgage rate (75% LTV) (Bank of England)
- uk.unemployment: UK unemployment rate (ONS)
- uk.pay-growth: UK regular pay growth (a year) (ONS)
- uk.gdp-growth: UK GDP growth (quarter on quarter) (ONS)
- uk.saving-ratio: UK households' saving ratio (ONS)
- uk.isa-allowance: ISA allowance (a year) (HMRC, 2025/26 tax year)
- uk.lisa-bonus: Lifetime ISA bonus (HMRC, 2025/26 tax year)
- uk.lisa-limit: Lifetime ISA limit (a year) (HMRC, 2025/26 tax year)
- uk.jisa-allowance: Junior ISA allowance (a year) (HMRC, 2025/26 tax year)
- uk.personal-allowance: Personal allowance (tax-free income) (HMRC, 2025/26 tax year)
- uk.pension-annual-allowance: Pension annual allowance (HMRC, 2025/26 tax year)
- uk.state-pension: Full new state pension (DWP, 2025/26 tax year)
- us.cpi: US inflation (CPI) (BLS via FRED)
- us.fed-rate: Fed funds rate (Federal Reserve via FRED)
- us.unemployment: US unemployment rate (BLS via FRED)
- us.mortgage-30: US 30-year mortgage rate (Freddie Mac via FRED)
- us.saving-rate: US personal saving rate (BEA via FRED)
- us.median-income: US median household income (Census via FRED)
- us.10y: US 10-year Treasury yield (Federal Reserve via FRED)
- us.sp500: S&P 500 (S&P Dow Jones via FRED)
- us.401k-limit: 401(k) contribution limit (IRS, 2025)
- us.ira-limit: IRA contribution limit (IRS, 2025)
