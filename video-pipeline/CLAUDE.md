# The Everyday Investor video pipeline

When a render finishes (a long video, a Short or a demo), run the `video-review` skill (.claude/skills/video-review)
before sending it to Jeffrey: the editor, the animator, the retention psychologist and the director review it
independently, the must-fix findings are fixed, at most two rounds. Before a long video's first build the director
plans the picture (the skill's step 0), and every new clip, drawing, pose or prop it asks for goes to Jeffrey.
