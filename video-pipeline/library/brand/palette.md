# The Everyday Investor: brand colours

Built from the logo (library/brand/logo.png: navy, white, a green arrow). Signed off by Jeffrey 2026-10-30 (no navy title card or outro: every page stays on the white board); the renderer's
`whiteboard` preset (remotion/src/theme.ts) uses these.

| Role | Hex | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Navy ink | `#041B3C` | every written word, outline, axis and chart line that is not a gain or a loss (replaces near-black) |
| Board | `#F8F9F8` | the page: a cool white, so the navy sits on it the way it does on the logo |
| Arrow green | `#01C77B` | fills, ticks, arrows, the solid "payoff" card, the progress line, the title card's accent |
| Green ink | `#0B8F58` | gains and figures written in words (the logo green is too light to read as text on white) |
| Loss red | `#D7263D` | fees, debt, falls, and the marker ring round a bad number. Red means a loss and nothing else |
| Highlighter | `#FFE86B` | a swipe under the key words, at about 75% |
| Slate | `#6B7A99` | labels, source lines, axes, anything the eye should pass over |
| Chart blue | `#2E6FE8` | the second line on a chart |
| Tints | `#EAFAF2` good news, `#FDEDEE` bad news (`tint=red`), `#EEF2F8` Jeffrey's own story | a faint page colour per section |

Rules
- Red is only ever a loss. The handwritten labels and notes, red today, become slate or navy.
- One accent for emphasis: the highlighter. The number ring is red for a bad figure and green for a good one.
- Photos and footage sit in a navy frame on the board, tilted a degree or two, with notes drawn on top in the
  same inks. Jeffrey's own photos and clips come first; stock second.
- The traced drawings stay black-line: next to navy ink they read as the same hand.
